## Item 1: K8
- Unknown usage is charged an estimate instead of failing closed: on reopen (in-flight attempts, and a record latched on
  `unknown_usage` by an older version), on a resend without usage (Working and Terminal), and on a rewind (Esc before first output:
  `Change::ChargeInFlight` from `finish_rewound_cancel`). Estimates are real debits, so a spent budget stops the goal with its typed
  `TotalTokensExhausted`/`OutputTokensExhausted`. A live record still fails closed (D.4). Late reports top an estimate up, never down.
- Memory filter: `fold()` maps look-alike letters (Cyrillic, Greek, small capitals) to Latin after NFKC.

## Item 2: K6
- `ModelsManager` counts catalog reloads (`catalog_reload_watch`) and `helper_epoch()`; `note_session_model_switch` covers Leader mode.
  Title client: `ReplaceSummaryHelper` to the persistence actor on a model switch and, via a bound watcher, on every reload.
  Classifier: `EpochCache` rebuilds the helper route when the epoch moves.
