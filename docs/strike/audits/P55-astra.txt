- **Bound but not listening → ECONNREFUSED:** Yes, on ordinary Linux IPv4 and IPv6 loopback. Binding reserves the endpoint without creating a listener; incoming SYNs trigger a reset, which the connecting socket reports as `ECONNREFUSED`. This does not repair the reported WSL mirrored IPv4 routing behavior—the existing candidate probe handles that distinction. See [Linux IPv4](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv4/tcp_ipv4.c), [IPv6](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv6/tcp_ipv6.c), and [reset handling](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv4/tcp_input.c).

- **Competing bind with SO_REUSEADDR:** Cannot take the reserved endpoint on Linux. Reuse requires cooperation from the existing socket; `closed_port` enables neither `SO_REUSEADDR` nor `SO_REUSEPORT`. A competing Tokio listener therefore gets `EADDRINUSE` for that endpoint, including an overlapping wildcard bind. The same numeric port can exist on a non-overlapping address without compromising the reservation. Relevant code: [loopback.rs:64](/Volumes/Mando/WaylandBots/Fuigo/wt-p55/crates/codegen/fuigo-test-support/src/loopback.rs:64). Sources: [socket(7)](https://www.man7.org/linux/man-pages/man7/socket.7.html), [kernel bind-conflict implementation](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv4/inet_connection_sock.c).

- **Ephemeral auto-bind:** Cannot steal the reserved endpoint. `bind(..., 0)` applies bind-conflict checks; unbound `connect()` skips the bind bucket marked by this explicit, non-reusable reservation. Exhausting the 4,096-port range causes allocation failure rather than overriding it. Sources: [port-zero allocation](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv4/inet_connection_sock.c), [connect allocation](https://github.com/torvalds/linux/blob/v6.6/net/ipv4/inet_hashtables.c).

Thus [loopback.rs:169](/Volumes/Mando/WaylandBots/Fuigo/wt-p55/crates/codegen/fuigo-test-support/src/loopback.rs:169) is valid outside a Tokio runtime.

The `_held` binding at [loopback.rs:124](/Volumes/Mando/WaylandBots/Fuigo/wt-p55/crates/codegen/fuigo-test-support/src/loopback.rs:124) owns the socket across the awaited probe; its underscore prefix does not drop it.
