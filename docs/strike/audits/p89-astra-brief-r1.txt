CONTEXT. A `/goal` (an autonomous multi-turn objective) runs its turns under ONE durable execution record,
keyed by blake3(session_id \0 goal_id) (crates/codegen/fuigo-shell/src/session/execution_state.rs). The record
mirrors limits: a model-call cap (from FUIGO_MAX_MODEL_CALLS), a deadline (FUIGO_MAX_RUNTIME_SECS), a goal
token budget, an output-token budget, `--max-turns` tool rounds. Before this change, any goal turn that did not
end EndTurn (user Esc, provider error, max-tokens cut, refusal) wrote phase Terminal to that record;
`/goal resume` keeps the goal id, so every later turn of the goal failed before reaching the model, forever.
An earlier packet (P03, commit 2cbcbd36) deliberately does NOT reopen a crash-poisoned Terminal record (one
whose receipt lists unresolved attempts/tool calls from a crash) and only clears its `abandoned` set.

THE CHANGE. (1) The turn now tells the record HOW it ended (`TurnEnd::{Succeeded, Interrupted, Stopped}`,
turn.rs `turn_end_of`, turn_end.rs for the dropped-future cancel path); the record stores `reopenable` when it
issues the receipt (`issue_terminal`). (2) A goal turn opens with `Execution::open_goal`, which reopens a
Terminal record (`Change::Reopen`) only if `may_reopen` (reopenable, or for pre-change records the
`legacy_reopenable` rule, AND no limit is spent per `exhaustion`). Reopen keeps counters, archives the receipt
in `earlier_receipts`, settles the interrupted turn's pending attempts/tool calls into that receipt. Otherwise
the open is refused with the budget's typed denial or `GoalExecutionEnded`. (3) `/goal resume` (goal.rs
`resume_goal`) refuses with the reason + remedy when the record cannot continue. (4) L-2: an execution that
never reaches `terminal()` gives its process-budget completion reservation back on Drop (ownership tracked in
`RESERVATION_OWNERS`), and `make_room` drops closed sessions' and the session's displaced execution before a
